e-visa

You can either apply online or apply for it on arrival (e-VOA). Either should work fine, but the queue at Bali airport could be long, so applying in advance can help avoid the line.

Planning Notes

  • Everyone should book international arrival flights to DPS rather than another Indonesian airport.
  • Everyone should book international departure flights from CGK.
  • Domestic flight estimates are shown in local currency (IDR) and Canadian dollars (CAD).
  • Allow sufficient connection time when booking the LBJ → YIA via DPS itinerary, particularly if separate tickets are involved.
  • Exact flight schedules, fares, and exchange rates may change when bookings are made.
