# Indonesia Trip — Jakarta Itinerary

## Overview

**3 nights in Jakarta**

Jakarta will be a lighter, more flexible portion of the trip, focused on historic neighborhoods, contemporary city life, and exploring different parts of the city.

**Accommodation:** Family home

---

## Day 11 — Yogyakarta → Jakarta

**Focus:** Arrival & settling in

- ✈️ Travel from Yogyakarta → Jakarta
- Settle in at family home
- Rest / catch up with family
- Optional casual evening outing depending on arrival time

**Overnight:** Family home

---

## Day 12 — Old Town + Glodok Chinatown

**Focus:** Historic Jakarta & Chinese-Indonesian culture

### Must-do
- 🏛️ **Jakarta Old Town / Kota Tua**
- **Fatahillah Square**
- Explore the surrounding historic streets and buildings
- 🏮 **Glodok Chinatown**
- Explore traditional shops, markets, and surrounding streets

### Optional
- Museums in Kota Tua, depending on interest and available time
- Additional exploration around Glodok

**Overnight:** Family home

---

## Day 13 — Blok M + Senayan

**Focus:** Contemporary Jakarta, neighborhoods & green space

### Must-do
- 🏙️ **Blok M**
- Explore the neighborhood, shops, streets, and surrounding areas
- 🏟️ **Gelora Bung Karno (GBK)**
- 🌳 **Hutan Kota GBK**

### Optional
- ☕ **Kemang** — café hopping
- 🛍️ **Pondok Indah Malls**
- 🛍️ **Grand Indonesia / Plaza Indonesia**

**Overnight:** Family home

> Blok M and Senayan will make up the main itinerary for the day. Kemang or a mall visit can be added depending on time and energy.

---

## Day 14 — Jakarta → Departure

**Focus:** Flexible final day

- Relaxed morning at family home
- Optional final neighborhood outing
- Optional shopping / mall visit if not already done
- ✈️ Head to the airport
