# Flight Logistics

## Overview

To keep the itinerary simple, everyone should plan their international arrival around **I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS)** in Bali and their international departure from **Soekarno-Hatta International Airport (CGK)** in Jakarta.

> **All estimated domestic flight prices below are shown in both Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) and Canadian Dollars (CAD).**

---

## International Arrival

**Arrival airport:**  
**I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS)** — Bali / Denpasar

## Domestic Flights

### Bali (DPS) → Labuan Bajo (LBJ)

**Route:** I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) → Komodo Airport (LBJ)

- ✈️ **Direct domestic flight**
- **Estimated price:** IDR 1,300,000–1,600,000
- **Approx. price in CAD:** $100–125 CAD

- **Estimated price:** IDR 2,800,000–3,400,000

- Everyone should book international arrival flights to **DPS** rather than another Indonesian airport.
- Everyone should book international departure flights from **CGK**.
