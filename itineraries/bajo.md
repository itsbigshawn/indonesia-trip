# Indonesia Trip — Labuan Bajo / Komodo Itinerary

## Labuan Bajo at a Glance

**3 nights in Labuan Bajo:** Labuan Bajo (1 night) → 2D/1N liveaboard (1 night) → Labuan Bajo (1 night)

The Labuan Bajo portion focuses on experiencing Komodo National Park through a short overnight sailing trip, while limiting time on the boat to one night given seasickness concerns.

| Day | Base | Main Experiences |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Labuan Bajo | Arrival, waterfront, sunset |
| 6 | Boat | Kelor Island, snorkeling, Kalong sunset |
| 7 | Labuan Bajo | Padar sunrise, Pink Beach, Komodo dragons |
| 8 | — | Labuan Bajo → Yogyakarta |

## Day 6 — 2D/1N Komodo Sailing Trip

**Focus:** Islands, snorkeling & sunset

Board the boat around midday/early afternoon.

### Main activities
- ⛵ Depart Labuan Bajo
- 🏝️ Kelor Island — easy walk, beach & snorkeling
- 🤿 Manjarite — snorkeling
- 🌅 Kalong Island — sunset and flying foxes
- 🍽️ Dinner on board
- 🌌 Overnight at anchor

**Overnight:** Boat

### Liveaboard Option

## Day 7 — Komodo → Labuan Bajo

**Focus:** Iconic Komodo experiences + return to Labuan Bajo

### Main activities
- 🌅 Padar Island sunrise
- 🏖️ Pink Beach
- 🦎 Komodo Island or Rinca Island — ranger-guided dragon trek
- 🤿 Snorkeling
- ⛵ Return to Labuan Bajo in the afternoon

### Evening
- Check back into hotel
- 🚿 Shower and relax
- 🍽️ Nice dinner
- 🌅 Optional sunset

**Overnight:** Labuan Bajo

> Keep the evening easy after two days on the boat. No major activity should be scheduled.
