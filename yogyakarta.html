Focus: Yogyakarta's iconic temples

Dedicated full-day sightseeing day

Morning — Borobudur

  • 🛕 Borobudur Temple
  • Explore the temple and surrounding complex
  • Take time to appreciate the architecture and Buddhist reliefs
  • Lunch after the visit

Afternoon — Travel to Prambanan

  • 🚗 Drive toward Prambanan
  • Stop for lunch / coffee on the way

Afternoon / Evening — Prambanan

  • 🛕 Prambanan Temple
  • Explore the temple complex
  • Stay into the late afternoon for the light / sunset if conditions and opening hours allow

Evening

  • Return to Yogyakarta
  • Dinner

Overnight: Yogyakarta

This will be the longest sightseeing day in Jogja, but since it's focused almost entirely on two major temple complexes, it should be manageable.
